FULL CURRICULUM

Comprehensive 60-Session Curriculum

Delivered across weekend sessions (Saturday & Sunday, two 1-hour sessions each day = 4 sessions/week for ~15 weeks).

Common Foundation Curriculum (34 Sessions)

Taken by both batches before track-specific domain training begins to ensure complete baseline industry readiness.

34 Sessions
Pharma Industry Overview (2 Sessions)
Drug Development Lifecycle (4 Sessions)
GxP Fundamentals - GMP/GCP/GLP (5 Sessions)
Documentation - SOP, BMR, TMF, CSR (6 Sessions)
Regulatory Basics - USFDA/CDSCO (5 Sessions)
Digital Skills - Excel & AI Tools (4 Sessions)
Data & Tech - EDC, MedDRA, CDISC (2 Sessions)
Quality & Ethics - ALCOA+ Data Integrity (2 Sessions)
Professional & Career Planning (4 Sessions)

Batch 1 — Clinical Track Domain Modules (26 Sessions)

Specialized domain training for freshers aiming at roles in clinical trial execution, data management, and SAS/R programming.

60 Sessions Total

Clinical Research (6 Sessions)

  • • Overview of drug development phases (I–IV) and clinical trial types
  • • ICH-GCP guidelines and ethical principles in research
  • • Informed Consent Document (ICD) preparation and presentation
  • • Protocol, Investigator's Brochure, and study design basics
  • • Site operations: patient screening, enrolment, and visit workflows

Clinical Data Management (7 Sessions)

  • • CRF/eCRF design and completion guidelines
  • • Data flow: collection, entry, cleaning, and query management
  • • Database design basics and edit checks
  • • Serious Adverse Event (SAE) data reconciliation & lock procedures

Clinical Programming (7 Sessions)

  • • CDISC standards overview (SDTM and ADaM)
  • • Basics of SAS/R programming for clinical datasets
  • • Generating Tables, Listings, and Figures (TLFs) walkthrough
  • • Data validation, quality checks, and Statistical Analysis Plans (SAP)

Industry Simulation Capstone (6 Sessions)

  • • End-to-end clinical workflow capstone project
  • • Applied CRF completion, query resolution & TMF filing
  • • Complete EDC system walkthrough and audit readiness

Batch 2 — Manufacturing & Compliance Track Modules (26 Sessions)

Specialized domain training for freshers aiming at roles in manufacturing, quality control, quality assurance, and regulatory affairs.

60 Sessions Total

Manufacturing (4 Sessions)

Solid, liquid & sterile dosage processes, cGMP principles, BMR execution, and line clearance controls.

Quality Control (4 Sessions)

Sampling, testing instrumentation (HPLC, UV, dissolution), stability studies, and OOS investigation.

Quality Assurance (4 Sessions)

Quality Management Systems (QMS), Deviation/CAPA management, SOP writing, and internal audits.

Regulatory Affairs (4 Sessions)

Global health bodies (CDSCO, USFDA, EMA), CTD/eCTD dossier compilation, and product registration.

Pharmacovigilance (4 Sessions)

Adverse event (ADR) reporting, ICSR processing, MedDRA coding basics, and signal detection (PSUR/PBRER).

Capstone Simulation (6 Sessions)

Batch documentation execution, audit-trail review, mock deviation/CAPA, and audit simulation.

CAREER OUTCOMES

Ten Pharmaceutical Career Pathways

Students are prepared to enter ten key pharmaceutical industry pathways, each mapped to industry-recognized skills and job roles.

CRA / CTA / Protocol Spec

Clinical Research

Design and support clinical trials that bring new therapies to patients globally.

RA Executive / Dossier Associate

Regulatory Affairs

Navigate approvals, dossier preparation (eCTD), and compliance across global health authorities.

QA Officer / Compliance Spec

Quality Assurance

Champion the quality management systems (QMS), SOPs, and CAPA processes that guarantee safety.

QC Analyst / Method Validation

Quality Control

Test and validate every batch using HPLC, GC, and wet chemistry against rigorous pharma standards.

Production Chemist / Formulation

Manufacturing

Operate, troubleshoot, and optimize modern pharmaceutical production and formulation lines.

Medical Writer / Protocol Editor

Medical Writing

Translate complex scientific findings into clear regulatory submissions, manuscripts, and protocols.

PV Scientist / Safety Data Spec

Pharmacovigilance

Monitor drug safety, process ICSRs, and manage post-marketing risk across product lifecycles.

Medical Coder / Data Reviewer

Medical Coding

Classify clinical trial adverse events and medical data accurately using MedDRA and WHO-DD.

CDM Associate / Data Coordinator

Clinical Data Mgmt.

Manage, clean, and safeguard the integrity of clinical trial databases and eCRFs.

Clinical Programmer / SAS Dev

Clinical Programming

Build skills in SAS, R, or Python programming for SDTM/ADaM clinical dataset transformation.
